## WYCKOFF THEORY IMPLEMENTATION

### THE FOUR PHASES TO DETECT

#### PHASE 1: ACCUMULATION
Wyckoff Accumulation has 5 sub-phases (A through E):

**Phase A — Stopping the downtrend:**
- PS (Preliminary Support): first sign of demand after prolonged downtrend
  - Detection: high volume bullish candle after 10+ candles of downtrend
  - Volume must be highest in last 20 candles
- SC (Selling Climax): panic selling exhaustion
  - Detection: wide-range bearish candle with volume spike (2x average)
  - Followed immediately by AR (Automatic Rally)
- AR (Automatic Rally): first bounce from SC
  - Detection: sharp bullish move of at least 3% from SC low
  - Establishes top of the trading range
- ST (Secondary Test): retest of SC area
  - Detection: price returns to SC area on LOWER volume than SC
  - Must hold above SC low to confirm accumulation

**Phase B — Building the cause:**
- Price oscillates between SC low (support) and AR high (resistance)
- Multiple tests of both boundaries
- Detection: at least 3 oscillations within the range
- Volume generally decreasing on tests
- Identify: SOW (Sign of Weakness) — minor breaks below support on low volume
- Identify: UA (Upthrust Action) — minor breaks above resistance on low volume

**Phase C — The Spring:**
- Spring: price breaks BELOW SC support briefly then reverses sharply back inside range
  - Detection: wick or close below range low followed by close back above
  - Volume on spring can be high or low (both valid)
  - Low volume spring = stronger (no supply)
  - This is the MOST IMPORTANT signal — institutional stop hunt before markup
- Test of Spring: retest of spring low on significantly lower volume
  - Detection: price approaches spring low, volume drops 50%+ vs spring candle

**Phase D — Markup beginning:**
- LPS (Last Point of Support): higher lows forming after spring
  - Detection: each pullback holds higher than previous low
- SOS (Sign of Strength): strong bullish move with increased volume
  - Detection: breaks above AR high with volume expansion
- BU (Back Up): retest of broken resistance (now support) — classic break and retest
  - Detection: price returns to former AR high level on low volume

**Phase E — Markup:**
- Price leaves the trading range with sustained upward movement
- Detection: close above AR high + 2 consecutive higher highs outside range

---

#### PHASE 2: DISTRIBUTION
Mirror of accumulation — detect institutional selling:

**Phase A — Stopping the uptrend:**
- PSY (Preliminary Supply): first sign of supply after prolonged uptrend
  - Detection: high volume bearish candle after 10+ candles of uptrend
- BC (Buying Climax): euphoric buying exhaustion
  - Detection: wide-range bullish candle with volume spike (2x average)
- AR (Automatic Reaction): first drop from BC
  - Detection: sharp bearish move of at least 3% from BC high
- ST (Secondary Test): retest of BC area on lower volume

**Phase B — Building the cause:**
- Price oscillates between AR low and BC high
- UT (Upthrust): break above BC high on low volume — false breakout
- SOW (Sign of Weakness): sharp drop within range on high volume

**Phase C — UTAD:**
- UTAD (Upthrust After Distribution): false breakout above BC high
  - Detection: close above range high followed by close back inside range within 3 candles
  - Volume spike on UTAD = strong distribution signal
  - This is the mirror of the Spring

**Phase D — Markdown beginning:**
- LPSY (Last Point of Supply): lower highs forming after UTAD
- SOW (Sign of Weakness): breaks below AR low with volume expansion
- Falls through former support levels

**Phase E — Markdown:**
- Sustained downward movement outside the range

---

#### PHASE 3: RE-ACCUMULATION
- Occurs mid-trend during markup phase
- Smaller consolidation pattern (range is 30-50% of initial accumulation range)
- Same sub-events as accumulation but compressed
- Detection: consolidation after 15%+ uptrend, spring occurs, then trend resumes
- Tag as: RE-ACCUMULATION

#### PHASE 4: RE-DISTRIBUTION
- Occurs mid-trend during markdown phase
- Mirror of re-accumulation
- Tag as: RE-DISTRIBUTION

**Effort vs Result:**
- High effort, high result = healthy trend (high volume + wide spread candle)
- High effort, low result = absorption (high volume + narrow spread = institutions absorbing)
- Low effort, high result = no resistance (low volume markup = easy move ahead)
- Low effort, low result = no interest (ignore these candles)

## TRADING RANGE DETECTION (wyckoff.py)

### Range Identification:
```python
def identify_trading_range(df, min_candles=15):
    """
    A trading range is a consolidation where:
    - Price oscillates between two boundaries for at least min_candles
    - Range high = highest high in the consolidation
    - Range low = lowest low in the consolidation
    - Range must be preceded by a trend (up or down) of at least 10 candles
    
    Returns:
    {
        range_high: float,
        range_low: float,
        range_midpoint: float,
        start_index: int,
        candle_count: int,
        preceding_trend: BULLISH | BEARISH,
        oscillation_count: int
    }
    """

def detect_spring(df, trading_range):
    """
    Spring = price closes below range_low then reverses above within 1-3 candles
    
    Spring strength:
    - Strong spring: low volume on break, closes well above range_low
    - Weak spring: high volume on break (supply present)
    
    Returns:
    {
        detected: bool,
        spring_index: int,
        spring_low: float,
        spring_volume: LOW | HIGH,
        strength: STRONG | WEAK,
        tested: bool,
        test_index: int (if tested)
    }
    """

def detect_utad(df, trading_range):
    """
    UTAD = price closes above range_high then reverses below within 1-3 candles
    Mirror of spring for distribution
    
    Returns same structure as detect_spring
    """

def classify_phase(df, trading_range, spring_result, utad_result, structure):
    """
    Determine current Wyckoff phase:
    
    Logic:
    - If preceding trend = BEARISH → potential ACCUMULATION
      - No spring yet → Phase A or B
      - Spring detected, not tested → Phase C
      - Spring tested, SOS forming → Phase D
      - Price outside range (above) → Phase E
    
    - If preceding trend = BULLISH → potential DISTRIBUTION
      - No UTAD yet → Phase A or B
      - UTAD detected → Phase C
      - LPSY forming → Phase D
      - Price outside range (below) → Phase E
    
    Returns:
    {
        phase_type: ACCUMULATION | DISTRIBUTION | RE-ACCUMULATION | RE-DISTRIBUTION,
        sub_phase: A | B | C | D | E,
        key_event: SPRING | SPRING_TESTED | SOS | LPS | UTAD | LPSY | SOW | BU,
        phase_confidence: LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH,
        bias: BULLISH | BEARISH | NEUTRAL,
        description: str  # human readable phase explanation
    }
    """
```
